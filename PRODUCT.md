# Product Context

## Product Name
Production Flow

## Product Purpose
An internal Kanban-style production board for tracking jobs from intake through completion. Staff log new jobs, move them through production stages, and monitor workload in real time.

## Register
product

## Users
- Internal operators and production staff.
- Users are working at a desk or on a production floor and need quick status reads and fast job entry.
- Not a customer-facing tool. No onboarding required; users are trained staff.

## Core Workflows
1. Create a new job record with client, deadline, resources, links, and production details.
2. Move jobs through statuses: New -> In Progress -> Waiting -> Done.
3. Track production stages: Artwork -> Printing -> Production -> Review -> Approval -> Installation.
4. View per-job history and the global activity feed.
5. Manage client, job type, and resource lists inline.

## Job Types
Design, Print, Website, Video, Admin, Installation, and user-added types.

## Brand
- Primary accent: yellow (`#FACC15` / Tailwind `yellow-400`).
- Tone: direct, functional, and easy to scan.
- Job IDs follow the `JOB-XXX` pattern.

## Strategic Principles
- Speed of use above all.
- The board should communicate workload at a glance.
- The interface should stay clear, structured, and practical.
